# A Security-Enhanced Conditional Privacy-Preserving CLAS for VANETs

## Overview

This paper gives an explicit forgery algorithm against Chen et al. CPP-CLAS [19] under public-key replacement by a Type-I adversary (§IV, pp.13485–13487), then proposes a security-enhanced pairing-free ECC CLAS proven EUF-CMA against Type-I/II adversaries in the random oracle model under ECDLP (§V, pp.13487–13491), plus a Binary Quick Search algorithm to identify invalid signatures after failed aggregate verification (Algorithm 1, p.13488).

## Relevance to Our Work

This scheme represents the "security-enhanced" generation of CLAS for VANETs. Its Chen-et-al. forgery case (Kl' = zl*P - h2l*Ppub key replacement) and its invalid-signature identification procedure provide design inputs for our own replacement-resistance and audit checks (§IV, pp.13486–13487).

## Key Contributions

- Concrete public-key-replacement forgery against Chen et al. CPP-CLAS (§IV, pp.13485–13487)
- Security-enhanced pairing-free CLAS with pseudonym conditional privacy (TA traceable) (§V, pp.13487–13489)
- Binary Quick Search for invalid signatures after aggregate-verify failure (Algorithm 1, p.13488)
- Formal EUF-CMA proof in ROM under ECDLP plus computation/communication comparison (§V–VII, pp.13489–13493)

## Mathematical Notation

Paper works over additive elliptic-curve group $G$ of prime order $q$ with generator $P$ on $E: y^2 = x^3 + ax + b \pmod p$ (§V Setup, p.13487); hashes $H_0$–$H_4$; KGC master $s$ with $P_{pub} = sP$; TA tracing secret $t$ with $T_{pub} = tP$; system params $\{G, q, T_{pub}, P_{pub}, H_0, H_1, H_2, H_3, H_4, P, G, E\}$ (§V, p.13487). No bilinear pairing is used.

### Setup (§V, p.13487)

$$
\begin{aligned}
&E: y^2 = x^3 + ax + b \pmod p,\quad G = \langle P \rangle,\ |G| = q \\
&s \in \mathbb{Z}_p^*,\quad P_{pub} = sP \\
&t \in \mathbb{Z}_p^*,\quad T_{pub} = tP \\
&H_0: \{0,1\}^* \times G \to \mathbb{Z}_p^*,\quad
H_1: \{0,1\}^* \times G \times G \to \mathbb{Z}_p^* \\
&H_2: \{0,1\}^* \times \{0,1\}^* \times \{0,1\}^* \times G \times G \times G \to \mathbb{Z}_p^* \\
&H_3: \{0,1\}^* \times \{0,1\}^* \times \{0,1\}^* \times G \times G \to \mathbb{Z}_p^* \\
&H_4: \{0,1\}^* \times \{0,1\}^* \times \{0,1\}^* \times G \to \mathbb{Z}_p^* \\
&\text{params} = \{G, q, T_{pub}, P_{pub}, H_0, H_1, H_2, H_3, H_4, P, G, E\}
\end{aligned}
$$

### Pseudonym Generation (TA) (§V, pp.13487–13488)

$$
\begin{aligned}
&v_i: f_i \in \mathbb{Z}_p^*,\ AID_{i,1} = f_i P,\ AID_{i,2} = f_i T_{pub},\ MID_i = VID_i \oplus AID_{i,2} \\
&TA: VID_i = MID_i \oplus tAID_{i,1},\ AID_{i,3} = H_0(tAID_{i,1}, \Delta T_i) \oplus VID_i \\
&PID_i = \{AID_{i,3}, \Delta T_i\}
\end{aligned}
$$

### Partial Private Key Extract (§V, p.13488)

$$
\begin{aligned}
&KGC: r_i \in \mathbb{Z}_p^*,\ R_i = r_i P \\
&h_{1i} = H_1(R_i, P_{pub}, PID_i),\quad d_i = r_i + s h_{1i} \\
&PPK_i = (d_i, R_i) \\
&\text{check: } d_i P = R_i + h_{1i} P_{pub}
\end{aligned}
$$

Note: the single/aggregate verification subsections list the H1 tuple in a different order, $H_1(PID_i, R_i, P_{pub})$, than the PPK-generation subsection $H_1(R_i, P_{pub}, PID_i)$ (§V, p.13488).

### Set Secret Value (§V, p.13488)

$$
\begin{aligned}
&x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_p^* \\
&X_i = x_i P
\end{aligned}
$$

### Set Private/Public Key (§V, p.13488)

$$
\begin{aligned}
&SK_i = (x_i, d_i) \\
&PK_i = (X_i, R_i)
\end{aligned}
$$

### Signature (§V, p.13488)

$$
\begin{aligned}
&v_i: k_i \in \mathbb{Z}_p^*,\ K_i = k_i P \\
&h_{2i} = H_2(PID_i, m_i, t_i, K_i, PK_i, P_{pub}) \\
&h_{3i} = H_3(PID_i, m_i, PK_i, K_i, h_{2i}) \\
&S_i = k_i + h_{2i} x_i + h_{3i} d_i \\
&\sigma_i = (K_i, S_i)
\end{aligned}
$$

Revocation extension only (§VII, p.13494): $S_i = k_i + h_{2i}x_i + h_{3i}d_i + h_{4i}z_i$ with $Z_i = z_i P$, $h_{4i} = H_4(PID_i, m_i, t_i, Z_i)$.

### Aggregate (CLAS) (§V, pp.13488–13489)

$$
\begin{aligned}
&S = \sum_{i=1}^{n} S_i,\quad K = \sum_{i=1}^{n} K_i \\
&\sigma = \{K, S\}
\end{aligned}
$$

RSU sends sigma plus {PKi, ti, sigmai, mi, PIDi} to AS (§V, pp.13488–13489).

### Verification (§V, p.13488)

Single (RSU), with h1i, h2i, h3i recomputed as above:

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\alpha_i = S_i P - K_i - h_{2i} X_i - h_{3i}(R_i + h_{1i} P_{pub}) \\
&\text{accept iff } \alpha_i = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

Aggregate (AS):

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\alpha = SP - K - \sum_{i=1}^{n} h_{2i} X_i - \sum_{i=1}^{n} h_{3i}(R_i + h_{1i} P_{pub}) \\
&\text{accept iff } \alpha = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

Correctness: $S_iP = (k_i + h_{2i} x_i + h_{3i} d_i)P = K_i + h_{2i} X_i + h_{3i}(R_i + h_{1i} P_{pub})$ (eq.2, §V.B, p.13488).

## Protocol / Scheme (§V, pp.13487–13489; Invalid-ID Algorithm 1, p.13488; extension §VII, p.13494)

1. **Setup**: TA/KGC publish curve params, $P_{pub}$, $T_{pub}$, $H_0$–$H_4$; keep $s, t$ secret (§V, p.13487)
2. **PseudonymGen**: vehicle/TA interactive AID/MID/PID issue with validity period $\Delta T_i$; TA stores $\{AID_{i,1}, PID_i, MID_i\}$ (§V, pp.13487–13488)
3. **PPKGen**: KGC issues $(d_i, R_i)$; vehicle checks $d_iP = R_i + h_{1i} P_{pub}$ (§V, p.13488)
4. **KeyGen**: vehicle sets $SK_i = (x_i, d_i)$, $PK_i = (X_i, R_i)$ (§V, p.13488)
5. **Sign**: random $k_i$, compute $K_i$, $h_{2i}$, $h_{3i}$, $S_i$ as above; send $\{PK_i, t_i, \sigma_i, m_i, PID_i\}$ (§V, p.13488)
6. **SingleVerify**: RSU checks $t_i$ freshness and $\Delta T_i$, recomputes $h_{1i}/h_{2i}/h_{3i}$, checks $\alpha_i = 0$ (§V, p.13488)
7. **Aggregate**: RSU sums $S, K$ over $n$ messages (§V, pp.13488–13489)
8. **AggVerify**: AS checks freshness, recomputes hashes, checks $\alpha = 0$ (§V, p.13489)
9. **Invalid-ID**: on $\alpha^* \neq 0$, AS+RSU run Binary Quick Search (Algorithm 1, p.13488) to isolate invalid sigs, send bad PIDs to TA for VID reveal (§V, pp.13488–13489)
10. **Extension**: mutual vehicle-RSU auth plus revocation key $z_i$ for revocability (§VII, p.13494)

## Security Analysis (§IV–VII, pp.13485–13492)

- Breaks Chen et al. [19]: Type-I adversary replaces $PK_l^*$ with $(R_l^*, K_l' = z_l^*P - h_{2l}^*P_{pub})$, forges $V_l^* = u_l^* + z_l^* h_{3l}^*$ passing $V^*P = U + \sum h_{3i}(K_i + h_{2i} P_{pub})$ (§IV, pp.13486–13487)
- EUF-CMA vs Type-I (Theorem 1) and Type-II (Theorem 2) in ROM under ECDLP via Game I/II simulation and Forking Lemma (§V, pp.13489–13491)
- Informal: message auth/integrity, non-repudiation, anonymity/unlinkability via pseudonyms, traceability/revocability via TA, replay resistance via $t_i$ and $\Delta T_i$ (§V, pp.13491–13492)
- Pairing-free: security reduces to ECDLP only; no BDH/bilinear assumption (§II–III, p.13484; §V, pp.13489–13491)

## Performance (§VI, pp.13491–13493)

- Single sign: 1 ECC mul $\approx 0.3218$ ms; single verify: 4 mul + 4 add $\approx 1.2968$ ms; aggregate verify: $(2n+2)$ mul + $(2n+2)$ add $\approx 0.6484n + 0.6484$ ms (p.13492; MIRACL on Windows-7 laptop, p.13491)
- Communication vehicle-to-RSU: $(PID_i, t_i, PK_i, m_i, \sigma_i)$ = $2|G| + 3|\mathbb{Z}_q^*| + 2|t| = 148$ bytes with $|G| = 40$, $|\mathbb{Z}_q^*| = 20$, $|t| = 4$ (p.13493)
- Compared applied-crypto timings; lower computation/communication than six related schemes [11]–[15],[18] per Tables IV–V and Figs. 3–6 (pp.13492–13493); Kumar et al. [12] aggregate-verify baseline $\approx 2.1866n + 20.4378$ ms cited for contrast (p.13492)

## Strengths & Limitations

- Strengths: explicit prior-scheme forgery, ROM proof, invalid-signature search, lowest reported computation (148-byte overhead) among compared schemes (§IV–VII, pp.13485–13493)
- Limitations: ROM only (no standard-model proof); revocation extension adds mutual-auth/key-agreement round not costed in main tables; TA/KGC trusted for trace and PPK issue (no decentralised tracing except future work); evaluation on dated Windows 7/MIRACL timings (§V–VII, pp.13487–13494)
