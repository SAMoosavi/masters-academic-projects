# A Lightweight CLAS without Pairing for VANETs

## Bibliographic
- Authors: Qiuling Yue, Weijian Jiang, Hong Lei | Year: 2025 | Venue: Scientific Reports 15:23663 | DOI: 10.1038/s41598-025-08656-1 | Received 16 January 2025, Accepted 23 June 2025

## Problem
Zheng et al.'s pairing-free CLAS for VANETs is vulnerable to temporary (ephemeral) rogue-key attacks in which an adversary exploits random nonces in signatures to generate an ephemeral rogue key. (p.1) The adversary forges an aggregate signature using this rogue key plus his/her own private key, and the forged signature passes verification undetected. (p.1) Existing pairing-free CLAS schemes remain exposed to such ephemeral rogue-key forgery despite avoiding certificate management overhead. (p.1) The paper fixes this vulnerability with an aggregator's-signature countermeasure and simultaneous verification of aggregator plus aggregate signatures. (p.1)

## Method
The scheme is built on elliptic-curve cryptography under ECDLP hardness with a fully trusted TA (identity recovery/pseudonyms) and an honest-but-curious non-colluding KGC (partial keys). (p.3, §System assumptions) System parameters are params = {P, q, E, G, H0, H1, H2, H3, H4, Kpub, Tpub} with five hashes H0–H4 mapping {0,1}* to Zq* and master keys a (KGC, Kpub = aP) and b (TA, Tpub = bP). (p.7) Vehicles pick xi, send Xi = xiP with TIDi = RIDi xor H0(xiTpub); the TA recovers RIDi = TIDi xor H0(bXi) and issues PIDi = RIDi xor H1(bXi, Ti, ti'). (p.7) The KGC picks ri, computes Ri = riP, H2i = H2(PIDi||Ri||Kpub||Xi||Ti), di = ri + aH2i, and sends Di = di xor H0(aXi) over a public channel for the vehicle to recover d'i = Di xor H0(Kpub xi). (p.7–p.8) Each vehicle signs with ui, Ui = uiP, H3i = H3(Mi||PIDi||PKi||Kpub||Ti||ti), H4i = H4(Mi||PIDi||PKi||Ui||Ti||ti), si = ui + d'iH3i + xiH4i, sigma_i = (Ui, si). (p.8) The aggregator (RSU) adds its own signature (U0 = u0P, H30, H40, s0 = u0 + d0H30 + H40x0, sigma_0 = (U0, s0)) over M0 = (Uset, PKset, PIDset) and outputs sigma = (U0..Un, s0, s) with s = sum_{i=0}^{n} si. (p.8–p.9) Verification is two-step: first s0P = U0 + (R0 + H20Kpub)H30 + H40X0, then sP = sum Ui + sum (Ri + KpubH2i)H3i + sum XiH4i + s0P. (p.9) Construction uses only scalar multiplication/addition and hashes to Zq*; no bilinear pairing e(...) appears in Sign/Aggregate/Verify. (p.8–p.9)

## Mathematical Notation

### Setup

$$
\begin{aligned}
&P \in \mathbb{G},\quad q \text{ is prime},\quad P \text{ generator of } \mathbb{G} \\
&H_0, H_1, H_2, H_3, H_4 : \{0,1\}^* \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}_q^* \\
&a, b \in \mathbb{Z}_q^* \quad (\text{KGC and TA master keys}) \\
&K_{pub} = aP,\quad T_{pub} = bP \\
&\text{params} = \{P, q, \mathbb{E}, \mathbb{G}, H_0, H_1, H_2, H_3, H_4, K_{pub}, T_{pub}\}
\end{aligned}
$$

### Pseudonym Identity Generation

$$
\begin{aligned}
&x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_q^*,\quad X_i = x_i P \\
&TID_i = RID_i \oplus H_0(x_i T_{pub}) \\
&TA: RID_i = TID_i \oplus H_0(b X_i) \\
&PID_i = RID_i \oplus H_1(b X_i, T_i, t'_i)
\end{aligned}
$$

### Partial Private Key Extract

$$
\begin{aligned}
&r_i \in \mathbb{Z}_q^*,\quad R_i = r_i P \\
&H_{2i} = H_2(PID_i \| R_i \| K_{pub} \| X_i \| T_i) \\
&d_i = r_i + a \cdot H_{2i} \\
&D_i = d_i \oplus H_0(a \cdot X_i)
\end{aligned}
$$

### Vehicle Key Generation

$$
\begin{aligned}
&d'_i = D_i \oplus H_0(K_{pub} \cdot x_i) \\
&H_{2i} = H_2(PID_i \| R_i \| K_{pub} \| X_i \| T_i \| t_i) \\
&\text{Verify: } d'_i P = R_i + H_{2i} \cdot K_{pub} \\
&SK_i = (x_i, d'_i),\quad PK_i = (X_i, R_i)
\end{aligned}
$$

### Signature

$$
\begin{aligned}
&u_i \in \mathbb{Z}_q^*,\quad U_i = u_i P \\
&H_{3i} = H_3(M_i \| PID_i \| PK_i \| K_{pub} \| T_i \| t_i) \\
&H_{4i} = H_4(M_i \| PID_i \| PK_i \| U_i \| T_i \| t_i) \\
&s_i = u_i + d'_i \cdot H_{3i} + x_i \cdot H_{4i} \\
&\sigma_i = (U_i, s_i)
\end{aligned}
$$

### Individual Verification

$$
\begin{aligned}
&s_i P \stackrel{?}{=} U_i + (R_i + H_{2i} \cdot K_{pub}) \cdot H_{3i} + X_i \cdot H_{4i}
\end{aligned}
$$

### Aggregate (with Aggregator's Signature)

$$
\begin{aligned}
&M_0 = (U_{set}, PK_{set}, PID_{set}) \\
&U_0 = u_0 P,\quad H_{30} = H_3(M_0 \| PID_0 \| PK_0 \| K_{pub} \| t_{set}) \\
&H_{40} = H_4(M_0 \| PID_0 \| PK_0 \| U_0 \| t_{set}) \\
&s_0 = u_0 + d_0 \cdot H_{30} + H_{40} \cdot x_0 \\
&s = \sum_{i=0}^{n} s_i \\
&\sigma = (U_0, U_1, \ldots, U_n, s_0, s)
\end{aligned}
$$

### Two-Step Aggregate Verification

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{Step 1 (Aggregator signature):} \\
&s_0 \cdot P \stackrel{?}{=} U_0 + (R_0 + H_{20} \cdot K_{pub}) \cdot H_{30} + H_{40} \cdot X_0 \\
\\
&\text{Step 2 (Aggregate signature):} \\
&s \cdot P \stackrel{?}{=} \sum_{i=1}^{n} U_i + \sum_{i=1}^{n}(R_i + K_{pub} \cdot H_{2i}) \cdot H_{3i} + \sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i \cdot H_{4i} + s_0 \cdot P
\end{aligned}
$$

## Protocol / Scheme

1. **Setup**: Two authorities — TA (key b, Tpub=bP) and KGC (key a, Kpub=aP). Five hash functions H₀–H₄.
2. **Pseudonym**: Vehicle picks xi, encrypts RID using TA's public key. TA decrypts, verifies, generates pseudonym PID.
3. **Partial Key**: KGC computes d_i = r_i + a·H2_i, XOR-encrypts D_i = d_i ⊕ H₀(a·X_i) over public channel.
4. **KeyGen**: Vehicle decrypts d'_i, verifies correctness, sets SK=(x_i, d'_i), PK=(X_i, R_i).
5. **Sign**: s_i = u_i + d'_i·H3_i + x_i·H4_i (two separate hash functions H3, H4).
6. **Aggregate**: Aggregator adds its own signature s_0, computes s = Σs_i, outputs σ = (U_0,...,U_n, s_0, s).
7. **Verify**: Two-step — first verify s_0·P, then verify s·P.

## Key findings
- The paper claims Table 1 pairing-free status for its scheme (✓) versus pairing-based Wang/Mei/Zhong/Kumar (✗), with ephemeral rogue-key resistance (✓) where Zheng et al. fail (✗). (p.2, Table 1)
- The fix is described as "simultaneous verification of both…" aggregator and aggregate signatures to block rogue-key forgery. (p.1)
- Security is argued for exactly two attacker types — key-replacing malicious vehicles (no KGC key) and malicious KGC (with KGC key, no key replacement) — plus selective/temporary rogue-key attackers with ephemeral keys; no Type-III (malicious-TA/coercion) model is stated. (p.9–p.10, §Security analysis)
- Individual Sign costs 2Th + Tpm and individual Verify 3Th + 3Tpa + 4Tpm; aggregate Verify costs 3(n+1)Th + 4(n+1)Tpa + (3n+5)Tpm with no Tbp/Thtp pairing terms. (p.11, Table 4)
- Communication per message is 3|G| + 3|Zq*| (Table 5) with |G| = 320 bits and |Zq*| = 160 bits (pairing |G1| = 1024 bits applies only to pairing-based competitors), timestamps omitted; the p.12 text value 4|G| + 3|Zq*| describes Zheng et al./Γ-signature∗ senders, not our row. (p.12–p.13, Table 5)
- The authors conclude the scheme is "unforgeable against two types of attackers…" under ECDLP with negligible aggregator-signature overhead. (p.14)

## Limitations
- The underlying CLS unforgeability proof is delegated to Zheng et al. rather than re-proven; only the XOR-protection and aggregator-signature delta plus rogue-key mitigation are argued directly. (p.10, §Security analysis)
- Aggregate verification is slightly costlier than Zheng et al. (extra s0 term: 3(n+1)Th + 4(n+1)Tpa + (3n+5)Tpm) as the price of the countermeasure. (p.11, Table 4)
- The threat model covers only the two stated attacker types and ephemeral rogue-key/selective-key attacks, with no Type-III adversary (e.g. malicious TA); TA is assumed fully trusted and never compromised. (p.3, p.9–p.10)
- KGC is assumed honest-but-curious and non-colluding, so collusion between KGC and malicious users is outside the proven model. (p.3, §System assumptions)

## Why it matters here
The audit question is whether Yue et al. claims Type-III and whether pairing inputs are correct. (p.9–p.10, p.11) The paper claims only Type-I-like plus Type-II-like unforgeability with ephemeral rogue-key resistance, so any Type-III attribution must be removed from our comparison tables. (p.9–p.10, §Security analysis) Pairing-table inputs for this row must use ECC-only costs (Th/Tpa/Tpm; 3|G| + 3|Zq*|) with no e(...) or Tbp terms, since e appears solely in the background calibration of pairing-based rivals. (p.11–p.13)
