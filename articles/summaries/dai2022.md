# Pairing-Free Certificateless Aggregate Signcryption for Vehicular Sensor Networks

## Overview

This paper proposes a pairing-free certificateless aggregate signcryption (CLASC) scheme that combines encryption and signature into a single operation for vehicular sensor networks (p.5063, Abstract). The scheme provides both confidentiality and authentication simultaneously while eliminating expensive bilinear pairing operations, making it suitable for resource-constrained vehicular environments (p.5063, Abstract). The CLASC approach is particularly relevant for scenarios where vehicles need to send encrypted safety messages that must also be authenticated. By combining signcryption with aggregate signatures, the scheme reduces both computation and communication overhead compared to separate encrypt-then-sign approaches (p.5063, §I-A).

## Key Contributions

- Pairing-free CLASC scheme for vehicular sensor networks, without bilinear pairings or MapToPoint hashes (p.5063, §I-A)
- Simultaneous sign + encrypt to reduce computational steps; aggregate n signatures to verify only once (p.5063, §I-A)
- ECC-based security analysis (confidentiality, unforgeability) in the random oracle model (p.5063, Abstract)
- Pseudonym-based conditional privacy with forward secrecy and known-attack resistance (p.5063, Abstract)

## Mathematical Notation

Paper-true construction with no bilinear pairing operator anywhere in the source (pp.5063-5072).

### Setup (Algorithm 1, p.5065)

$$
\begin{aligned}
&G \text{ cyclic group of prime order } q \text{ with generator } P \\
&s \in \mathbb{Z}_q^*,\quad P_{Pub} = sP \\
&H_1, H_2, H_3: \{0,1\}^* \rightarrow \mathbb{Z}_q^* \\
&params = \{G, q, P, P_{Pub}, H_1, H_2, H_3\}
\end{aligned}
$$

### Partial Private Key Extract (Algorithm 2, p.5065)

$$
\begin{aligned}
&y_{ID} \in_R \mathbb{Z}_q^*,\quad Y_{ID} = y_{ID}P \\
&h_{ID} = H_1(ID, Y_{ID}, P_{Pub}) \\
&d_{ID} = y_{ID} + h_{ID}\, s \pmod q
\end{aligned}
$$

Partial-key sanity check used at registration (p.5067, §IV-C):

$$
d_{ID}P = Y_{ID} + h_{ID}P_{Pub}
$$

### Set Secret Value (Algorithm 3, p.5065)

$$
\begin{aligned}
&x_{ID} \in_R \mathbb{Z}_q^*,\quad X_{ID} = x_{ID}P \\
&K_{ID} = X_{ID} + Y_{ID} \\
&sk_{ID} = (x_{ID}, d_{ID}),\quad PK_{ID} = (K_{ID}, Y_{ID})
\end{aligned}
$$

### Signcrypt (Algorithm 4, p.5066)

$$
\begin{aligned}
&u_{i,1}, u_{i,2} \in_R \mathbb{Z}_q^*,\quad u_i = (u_{i,1}\,x_{ID_i} + u_{i,2}\,d_{ID_i})(x_{ID_i} + d_{ID_i})^{-1} \\
&U_i = u_iP \\
&h_{ID_B} = H_1(ID_B, Y_{ID_B}, P_{Pub}) \\
&K_i = u_i\,(K_{ID_B} + h_{ID_B}P_{Pub}) \\
&z_i = H_2(ID_B, U_i, K_i, t_i) \\
&c_i = m_i \oplus z_i \\
&h_i = H_3(m_i, ID_i, PK_{ID_i}, U_i, K_i, t_i) \\
&w_i = u_i + h_i\,(x_{ID_i} + d_{ID_i}) \pmod q \\
&\sigma_i = (U_i, c_i, w_i)
\end{aligned}
$$

### Unsigncrypt / Individual Verification (Algorithm 5, p.5066)

Receiver recomputes (DEC, p.5066):

$$
K_i = (x_{ID_B} + d_{ID_B})\,U_i,\quad m_i = c_i \oplus z_i
$$

Paper-true ECC verification equation (Algorithm 5, line 6, p.5066):

$$
w_i P \stackrel{?}{=} U_i + h_i\,(K_{ID_i} + h_{ID_i}P_{Pub})
$$

### Aggregate (Algorithm 6, p.5066)

$$
W = \sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i,\quad \sigma = (\{U_i, c_i \mid 1 \le i \le n\}, W)
$$

### Aggregate Verification (Algorithm 7, line 5, p.5066)

$$
W P \stackrel{?}{=} \sum_{i=1}^{n} U_i + \sum_{i=1}^{n} h_i\,(K_{ID_i} + h_{ID_i}P_{Pub})
$$

## Protocol / Scheme

1. **Setup**: KGC initialization (p.5065, Algorithm 1)
2. **KeyGen**: Partial-key extract + secret-value generation (p.5065, Algorithms 2-3)
3. **Signcrypt**: Encrypt and sign message simultaneously (p.5066, Algorithm 4)
4. **Aggregate Signcrypt**: Combine multiple signcryptions into W (p.5066, Algorithm 6)
5. **Unsigncrypt + Verify**: Decrypt and verify individual (p.5066, Algorithm 5) or aggregate (p.5066, Algorithm 7)

## Security Analysis

- IND-CCA2 confidentiality under the CDH assumption in the ROM (p.5067, Theorem 1)
- EUF-CMA unforgeability under the ECDL assumption in the ROM (p.5069, Theorem 2)
- Forward secrecy, attack resistance (MITM/DoS/replay/ESL), and pseudonym-based conditional traceability (p.5070, §V-B)
- ProVerif validation of key/message delivery under the Dolev-Yao model (p.5069, §V-A)

## Performance

- Eliminates bilinear pairing operations (p.5063, §I-A)
- Single aggregate verification for n signcryptions; e.g. n=350 aggregate unsigncryption takes 466.4302 ms (p.5071, §VI)
- Lower computation and communication overhead than state-of-the-art schemes (p.5063, Abstract)

## Strengths & Limitations

- Strengths: Efficient combined operation, practical for sensor networks (p.5063, Abstract)
- Limitations: Evaluation assumes 160-bit elements/messages on MIRACL/I7-4770 and DSRC traffic of ~350 messages per second (pp.5070-5071, §VI-B)
