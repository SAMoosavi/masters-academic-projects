# Pairing-Free Certificateless Aggregate Signcryption for Vehicular Sensor Networks

## Overview

This paper proposes a pairing-free certificateless aggregate signcryption (CLASC) scheme that combines encryption and signature into a single operation for vehicular sensor networks. The scheme provides both confidentiality and authentication simultaneously while eliminating expensive bilinear pairing operations, making it suitable for resource-constrained vehicular environments. The CLASC approach is particularly relevant for scenarios where vehicles need to send encrypted safety messages that must also be authenticated. By combining signcryption with aggregate signatures, the scheme reduces both computation and communication overhead compared to separate encrypt-then-sign approaches.

## Relevance to Our Work

This paper represents the signcryption variant of CLAS. While our work focuses on pure aggregate signatures, the signcryption approach is relevant for VANET scenarios requiring confidentiality. It's also the target of Wu & Chen (2025)'s cryptanalysis.

## Key Contributions

- First pairing-free CLASC scheme for vehicular sensor networks
- Combines encryption and signature in single operation
- Aggregate signcryption for multiple messages
- ECC-based construction avoiding bilinear pairings

## Mathematical Notation

### Setup

$$
\begin{aligned}
&s \in \mathbb{Z}_q^*,\quad P_{pub} = sP \\
&H:\{0,1\}^*\rightarrow\mathbb{Z}_q^*
\end{aligned}
$$

### Partial Private Key Extract

$$
\begin{aligned}
&D_i = sH_1(ID_i)
\end{aligned}
$$

### Set Secret Value

$$
\begin{aligned}
&x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_q^* \\
&PK_{receiver} = x_{receiver}P
\end{aligned}
$$

### Signature

$$
\begin{aligned}
&k_i \in \mathbb{Z}_q^*,\quad R_i = k_iP \\
&K = k_i \cdot PK_{receiver} \\
&c = m \oplus H(K) \\
&\sigma_i = k_i + H(m \| ID_i \| R_i) \cdot x_i + D_i \pmod q
\end{aligned}
$$

### Aggregate (CLAS)

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\sigma_{agg} = \sum_{i=1}^n \sigma_i
\end{aligned}
$$

### Verification

$$
\begin{aligned}
e(\sigma_{agg}P, P) \stackrel{?}{=} e\left(\sum R_i, P\right) \cdot e\left(\sum H(m_i \| ID_i \| R_i) \cdot PK_i + H_1(ID_i), P_{pub}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

## Protocol / Scheme

1. **Setup**: KGC initialization
2. **KeyGen**: Vehicle key generation (partial + secret value)
3. **Signcrypt**: Encrypt and sign message simultaneously
4. **Aggregate Signcrypt**: Combine multiple signcryptions
5. **Unsigncrypt + Verify**: Decrypt and verify aggregate

## Security Analysis

- Confidentiality under chosen ciphertext attack
- Unforgeability under chosen message attack
- Pairing-free construction reduces computation

## Performance

- Eliminates bilinear pairing operations
- Single aggregate for multiple signcryptions
- Lower overhead than separate sign + encrypt

## Strengths & Limitations

- Strengths: Efficient combined operation, practical for sensor networks
- Limitations: Vulnerable to public key replacement attacks (shown by Wu & Chen 2025)
