# A Comprehensive Review of ECC-based CLAS for VANETs and Cryptanalysis of Quantum-Secure CLAS

## Overview

This paper provides a critical survey of pairing-free CLAS schemes based on ECC for VANETs, covering the full landscape of ECC-based approaches. The authors go beyond a simple review by performing rigorous security analysis of a recent quantum-secure CLAS scheme, exposing a crucial weakness that undermines its claimed quantum resistance. The review focuses on how CLAS combines multiple signatures from different vehicles into a single short signature, keeping verification cost low. The cryptanalysis of the quantum-secure scheme is particularly significant as it challenges the assumption that existing post-quantum CLAS constructions are secure.

## Relevance to Our Work

This is the most recent comprehensive review (2026), covering literature our article must cite. Its quantum-secure cryptanalysis is directly relevant to future-proofing CLAS schemes. The ECC-based taxonomy helps position our contribution.

## Key Contributions

- Comprehensive survey of ECC-based CLAS for VANETs (pairing-free focus)
- Cryptanalysis of quantum-secure CLAS scheme — exposes critical weakness
- Security taxonomy of ECC-based approaches
- Critical analysis of post-quantum CLAS claims

## Mathematical Notation

### Setup

$$
\begin{aligned}
&(E/\mathbb{F}_p, P, q, H) \\
&s \in \mathbb{Z}_q^*,\quad P_{pub} = sP
\end{aligned}
$$

### Partial Private Key Extract

$$
\begin{aligned}
&D_i = sH_1(ID_i)
\end{aligned}
$$

### Set Secret Value

$$
\begin{aligned}
&x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_q^* \\
&PK_i = x_iP
\end{aligned}
$$

### Signature

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\sigma_i = r_i + h_i(x_i + D_i) \pmod q
\end{aligned}
$$

### Aggregate (CLAS)

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\sigma = \sum \sigma_i
\end{aligned}
$$

### Verification

$$
\begin{aligned}
e(\sigma P, P) \stackrel{?}{=} e\left(\sum R_i, P\right) \cdot e\left(\sum h_i(PK_i) + H_1(ID_i), P_{pub}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$

## Protocol / Scheme

1. **Pairing-based CLAS**: Uses bilinear maps $e: G_1 \times G_1 \to G_2$
2. **Pairing-free ECC CLAS**: Uses only scalar multiplication on elliptic curves
3. **HECC-based CLAS**: Uses hyperelliptic curves for smaller keys
4. **Quantum-resistant CLAS**: Attempts lattice/hash-based approaches (found vulnerable)

## Security Analysis

- Extensive taxonomy of attacks: Type I, II, III, forgery, KGC, replay
- Critical analysis of quantum-secure scheme: identified algebraic weakness
- Most schemes resist only Type I and II; Type III resistance rare
- Standard model vs. ROM trade-offs analyzed

## Performance

- Comparison tables of computation/communication overhead
- ECC pairing-free consistently most efficient for VANETs
- HECC offers smaller keys but less practical deployment

## Strengths & Limitations

- Strengths: Most up-to-date review (2026), quantum-secure cryptanalysis, comprehensive taxonomy
- Limitations: Survey-based, doesn't propose a new scheme
