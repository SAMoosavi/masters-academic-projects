[[xu2023]]

Pairing-free ECC CLAS (no bilinear pairing); all operations are scalar multiplication / point addition on $G$ (§V, pp.13487–13489).

## Setup (§V, p.13487)

$$
\begin{aligned}
&E: y^2 = x^3 + ax + b \pmod p,\quad G = \langle P \rangle,\ |G| = q \\
&s \in \mathbb{Z}_p^*,\quad P_{pub} = sP \\
&t \in \mathbb{Z}_p^*,\quad T_{pub} = tP \\
&H_0: \{0,1\}^* \times G \to \mathbb{Z}_p^*,\quad
H_1: \{0,1\}^* \times G \times G \to \mathbb{Z}_p^* \\
&H_2: \{0,1\}^* \times \{0,1\}^* \times \{0,1\}^* \times G \times G \times G \to \mathbb{Z}_p^* \\
&H_3: \{0,1\}^* \times \{0,1\}^* \times \{0,1\}^* \times G \times G \to \mathbb{Z}_p^* \\
&H_4: \{0,1\}^* \times \{0,1\}^* \times \{0,1\}^* \times G \to \mathbb{Z}_p^*
\end{aligned}
$$

Pseudonym: $PID_i = \{AID_{i,3}, \Delta T_i\}$ with $AID_{i,1} = f_i P$, $MID_i = VID_i \oplus f_i T_{pub}$, $AID_{i,3} = H_0(tAID_{i,1}, \Delta T_i) \oplus VID_i$ (§V, pp.13487–13488).

## Partial Private Key Extract (§V, p.13488)

$$
\begin{aligned}
&r_i \in \mathbb{Z}_p^*,\quad R_i = r_i P \\
&h_{1i} = H_1(R_i, P_{pub}, PID_i) \\
&d_i = r_i + s h_{1i} \\
&PPK_i = (d_i, R_i),\quad \text{check: } d_i P = R_i + h_{1i} P_{pub}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$

## Set Private/Public Key (§V, p.13488)

$$

## Signature (§V, p.13488)

$$
\begin{aligned}
&k_i \in \mathbb{Z}_p^*,\quad K_i = k_i P \\
&h_{2i} = H_2(PID_i, m_i, t_i, K_i, PK_i, P_{pub}) \\
&h_{3i} = H_3(PID_i, m_i, PK_i, K_i, h_{2i}) \\
&S_i = k_i + h_{2i} x_i + h_{3i} d_i \\
&\sigma_i = (K_i, S_i)
\end{aligned}
$$

## Aggregate (§V, pp.13488–13489)

$$
\begin{aligned}
&S = \sum_{i=1}^{n} S_i,\quad K = \sum_{i=1}^{n} K_i \\
&\sigma = \{K, S\}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Verification (§V, pp.13488–13489)

Single (RSU):

$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha_i = S_i P - K_i - h_{2i} X_i - h_{3i}(R_i + h_{1i} P_{pub}),\quad \text{accept iff } \alpha_i = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

Aggregate (AS):

$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha = SP - K - \sum_{i=1}^{n} h_{2i} X_i - \sum_{i=1}^{n} h_{3i}(R_i + h_{1i} P_{pub}),\quad \text{accept iff } \alpha = 0
\end{aligned}
$$

Correctness: $S_iP = (k_i + h_{2i} x_i + h_{3i} d_i)P = K_i + h_{2i} X_i + h_{3i}(R_i + h_{1i} P_{pub})$ (eq.2, §V.B, p.13488).

## Notes (§V, p.13488; §VII, p.13494)

- Invalid signatures after an $\alpha^* \neq 0$ aggregate failure are isolated with the Binary Quick Search (Algorithm 1, p.13488); bad PIDs go to TA for identity reveal (§V, pp.13488–13489).
- Revocation extension only: $Z_i = z_i P$, $h_{4i} = H_4(PID_i, m_i, t_i, Z_i)$, $S_i = k_i + h_{2i} x_i + h_{3i} d_i + h_{4i} z_i$ (§VII, p.13494).
