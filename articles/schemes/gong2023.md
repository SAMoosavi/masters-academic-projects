All equations below are ECC-only (scalar multiplication and point addition, no bilinear pairing), per PCAS §5.2 (p.7–8, §5.2).

## Setup

System Initialization publishes `params = {q, P, G, P_pub, T_pub, H, H1–H4}` with `T_pub = s1*P`, `P_pub = s2*P` (p.7, §5.2).

$$
\begin{aligned}
&P \in \mathbb{G},\quad s_1,s_2\in\mathbb{Z}_q^* \\
&T_{pub}=s_1 P,\quad P_{pub}=s_2 P \\
&H:\mathbb{G}\times\mathbb{G}\rightarrow\{0,1\}^* \\
&H_1:\mathbb{G}\times\{0,1\}^*\times\{0,1\}^*\times\mathbb{G}\times\mathbb{G}\rightarrow\mathbb{Z}_q^* \\
&H_2:\mathbb{G}\times\{0,1\}^*\times\{0,1\}^*\times\mathbb{G}\rightarrow\mathbb{Z}_q^* \\
&H_3:\{0,1\}^*\times\mathbb{G}\times\{0,1\}^*\times\{0,1\}^*\times\mathbb{G}\rightarrow\mathbb{Z}_q^* \\
&H_4:\{0,1\}^*\times\mathbb{G}\times\{0,1\}^*\times\{0,1\}^*\times\mathbb{G}\times\{0,1\}^*\rightarrow\mathbb{Z}_q^*
\end{aligned}
$$

## Partial Private Key Extract

KGC picks `r_i`, sets `R_i = r_i*P`, and issues `psk = (d, R)` verifiable by `d*P = R + h1*P_pub` (p.7–8, §5.2).

$$
\begin{aligned}
&r_i\in\mathbb{Z}_q^*,\quad R_i=r_i P \\
&h_{1i,j}=H_1(pseu_{i,j},R_i,P_{pub}) \\
&d_{i,j}=r_i+s_2\cdot h_{1i,j} \\
&psk_{i,j}=(d_{i,j},R_i)
\end{aligned}
$$

## Set Secret Value

Each vehicle picks its own secret `x_i` and publishes `X_i = x_i*P` (p.8, §5.2).

$$
\begin{aligned}
&x_i\in\mathbb{Z}_q^* \\
&X_i=x_i P
\end{aligned}
$$

## Set Private/Public Key

The vehicle binds its secret via `alpha = H2(pseu, X)` and `D = R + alpha*X`, keeping `(d, x)` secret (p.8, §5.2).

$$
\begin{aligned}
&SK_{i,j}=(d_{i,j},x_i) \\
&PK_{i,j}=(D_{i,j},R_i) \text{ where } D_{i,j}=R_i+\alpha_{i,j}X_i,\ \alpha_{i,j}=H_2(pseu_{i,j},X_i)
\end{aligned}
$$

## Signature

Signing picks fresh `y_{1i}`, forms `Y_{1i} = y_{1i}*P`, and computes the scalar `w_i` with no pairing operator (p.8, §5.2).

$$
\begin{aligned}
&y_{1i}\in\mathbb{Z}_q^* \\
&Y_{1i}=y_{1i}P \\
&\alpha_{i,j}=H_2(pseu_{i,j},X_i) \\
&h_{3i}=H_3(m_i,pseu_{i,j},Y_{1i}) \\
&h_{4i}=H_4(m_i,pseu_{i,j},PK_{i,j},t_i) \\
&w_i=[h_{3i}(d_{i,j}+\alpha_{i,j}\cdot x_i)+y_{1i}\cdot h_{4i}]\pmod q \\
&\sigma_i=(Y_{1i},w_i)
\end{aligned}
$$

## Aggregate

The RSU weights each `Y_{1i}` with its `h_{4i}` and sums the scalars, outputting `sigma = (Y, w)` (p.8, §5.2).

$$
\begin{aligned}
&Y=\sum_{i=1}^n h_{4i}\cdot Y_{1i},\quad w=\sum_{i=1}^n w_i \\
&\sigma=(Y,w)
\end{aligned}
$$

## Verification

Single verification checks Eq. (1) and aggregate verification checks Eq. (2), using only ECC scalar multiplication and point addition (p.8, §5.2).

$$
\begin{aligned}
&w_iP-h_{4i}Y_{1i}\stackrel{?}{=}h_{3i}D_{i,j}+h_{1i,j}P_{pub} \\
&wP-Y\stackrel{?}{=}\sum_{i=1}^n\left(h_{3i}\cdot D_{i,j}+h_{1i,j}\cdot P_{pub}\right)
\end{aligned}
$$
