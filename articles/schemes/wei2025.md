[[wei2025]]

Scope: IoT-CLS single-signer (not aggregate, not VANET). Pairing-free: ECC scalar mult, point addition, hashes only.

## Setup

$$
\begin{aligned}
&P \in \mathbb{G},\quad q=|\mathbb{G}|,\quad s\leftarrow\mathbb{Z}_q^* \\
&P_{pub}=sP \\
&H_1:\{0,1\}^*\times\mathbb{G}^3\rightarrow\mathbb{Z}_q^*,\quad H_2:\{0,1\}^{*2}\times\mathbb{G}^3\rightarrow\mathbb{Z}_q^*
\end{aligned}
$$

## Set Secret Value

$$
\begin{aligned}
&x_{ID}\leftarrow\mathbb{Z}_q^* \\
&X_{ID}=x_{ID}P
\end{aligned}
$$

## Partial Private Key Extract

$$
\begin{aligned}
&r_{ID}\leftarrow\mathbb{Z}_q^*,\quad R_{ID}=r_{ID}P \\
&h_1=H_1(ID,X_{ID},R_{ID},P_{pub}) \\
&d_{ID}=r_{ID}+h_1\cdot s
\end{aligned}
$$

## Set Private/Public Key

$$
\begin{aligned}
&SK_{ID}=(x_{ID},d_{ID}) \\
&PK_{ID}=(X_{ID},R_{ID})
\end{aligned}
$$

## Signature

$$
\begin{aligned}
&t\leftarrow\mathbb{Z}_q^*,\quad T=tP \\
&h_2=H_2(m,ID,X_{ID},R_{ID},T) \\
&v=t+h_2(x_{ID}+d_{ID})\pmod q \\
&\tau=(T,v)
\end{aligned}
$$

## Verification

$$
\begin{aligned}
&h_1=H_1(ID,X_{ID},R_{ID},P_{pub}) \\
&h_2=H_2(m,ID,X_{ID},R_{ID},T) \\
&vP\stackrel{?}{=}T+h_2(X_{ID}+R_{ID}+h_1\cdot P_{pub})
\end{aligned}
$$
