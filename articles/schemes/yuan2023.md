## Setup

KGC/TRA generate prime order `q > 2^ν`, groups `G1, G2`, bilinear map `e: G1 × G1 → G2`, five hashes `H1–H5`, master key `s` with `y_pub = sP` and system key `Z = H3(y_pub)`; TRA picks tracking key `k` with `K = kP` (p.7–8, §5).

$$
\begin{aligned}
&e: G_1 \times G_1 \to G_2,\quad q > 2^{\nu} \\
&P \in G_1,\quad s \in \mathbb{Z}_q^*,\quad y_{pub} = sP,\quad Z = H_3(y_{pub}) \\
&H_1: G_1 \to \mathbb{Z}_q^*,\quad H_2: \{0,1\}^* \times G_1 \times G_1 \to \mathbb{Z}_q^* \\
&H_3: G_1 \to G_1,\quad H_4: G_1^4 \to \mathbb{Z}_q^*,\quad H_5: \{0,1\}^* \times \{0,1\}^* \times G_1^5 \to \mathbb{Z}_q^* \\
&params = \{G_1, G_2, q, e, P, Z, y_{pub}, H_1, H_2, H_3, H_4, H_5\}
\end{aligned}
$$

## Partial Private Key Extract

KGC picks `r_i`, sets `R_i = r_i P`, derives `k_i = H2(PIDi,j, R_i)` and issues `d_i = r_i + k_i s mod q` over a trusted route (p.8, §5).

$$
\begin{aligned}
&r_i \in \mathbb{Z}_q^*,\quad R_i = r_i P \\
&k_i = H_2(PID_{i,j}, R_i) \\
&d_i = r_i + k_i \cdot s \bmod q
\end{aligned}
$$

## Set Secret Value

Each vehicle picks its own secret `x_i` and computes `X_i = x_i P` (p.8, §5).

$$
\begin{aligned}
&x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_q^* \\
&X_i = x_i P
\end{aligned}
$$

## Set Private/Public Key

The vehicle publishes `PKi = (X_i, R_i)` and keeps `(d_i, x_i)` as the private key (p.8, §5).

$$
\begin{aligned}
&SK_i = (d_i, x_i) \\
&PK_i = (X_i, R_i)
\end{aligned}
$$

## Signature

OBU picks timestamp `TSi` and fresh `u_i`, forms `U_i = u_i P`, binds `φi = H4(y_pub, PKi, Ui)` and `hi = H5(mi || TSi, PIDi,j, Ui, PKi)`, and outputs `σi = (U_i, W_i)`; each pseudonym is single-use (p.8, §5).

$$
\begin{aligned}
&u_i \in \mathbb{Z}_q^*,\quad U_i = u_i P \\
&\varphi_i = H_4(y_{pub}, PK_i, U_i) \\
&h_i = H_5(m_i \| TS_i, PID_{i,j}, U_i, PK_i) \\
&W_i = (d_i \cdot \varphi_i + h_i \cdot x_i + u_i) \cdot Z \\
&\sigma_i = (U_i, W_i)
\end{aligned}
$$

## Aggregate

RSU sums `n` individual pairs into `U = ΣU_i`, `W = ΣW_i` and sends `σ = (U, W)` to the TMC (p.9, §5).

$$
\begin{aligned}
&U = \sum_{i=1}^n U_i,\quad W = \sum_{i=1}^n W_i \\
&\sigma = (U, W)
\end{aligned}
$$

## Verification

Single check is equation (3) after recomputing `k_i, φi, h_i` and testing timestamp freshness; aggregate check is equation (4) over recomputed values plus freshness of every `TSi` (p.8–9, §5).

$$
\begin{aligned}
&\text{Single (3):}\quad e(W_i, P) \stackrel{?}{=} e((R_i + k_i \cdot y_{pub}) \cdot \varphi_i + h_i \cdot X_i + U_i, Z) \\
&\text{Aggregate (4):}\quad e(W, P) \stackrel{?}{=} e\left(\sum_{i=1}^n (R_i + k_i \cdot y_{pub}) \varphi_i + \sum_{i=1}^n h_i \cdot X_i + U, Z\right)
\end{aligned}
$$
