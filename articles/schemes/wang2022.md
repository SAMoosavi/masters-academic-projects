## Setup

$$
\begin{aligned}
&P \in \mathbb{G},\quad \alpha\in\mathbb{Z}_q^* \\
&P_{pub}=\alpha P \\
&H_1:\{0,1\}^*\rightarrow\mathbb{G},\quad H_2,H_3:\{0,1\}^*\rightarrow\mathbb{Z}_q^*
\end{aligned}
$$

## Partial Private Key Extract

$$
\begin{aligned}
&d_i=\alpha\cdot H_1(ID_i,P_i)
\end{aligned}
$$

## Set Secret Value

$$
\begin{aligned}
&x_i\in\mathbb{Z}_q^* \\
&P_i=x_i P
\end{aligned}
$$

## Set Private/Public Key

$$
\begin{aligned}
&SK_i=(d_i,x_i) \\
&PK_i=P_i
\end{aligned}
$$

## Signature

$$
\begin{aligned}
&r_i\in\mathbb{Z}_q^*,\quad R_i=r_i P \\
&h_i=H_2(ID_i,m_i,P_i,R_i) \\
&S_i=(d_i+x_i h_i+r_i)\cdot H_3(ID_i) \\
&\sigma_i=(R_i,S_i)
\end{aligned}
$$

## Aggregate

$$
\begin{aligned}
&S=\prod_{i=1}^n S_i \\
&\sigma=S
\end{aligned}
$$

## Verification

$$
\begin{aligned}
e(S,P)\stackrel{?}{=}\prod_{i=1}^n\Bigl[e(d_i\cdot H_3(ID_i),P)\cdot e(H_3(ID_i),P_{pub})\cdot e(h_i\cdot H_3(ID_i),P_i)\cdot e(H_3(ID_i),R_i)\Bigr]
\end{aligned}
$$
