# CLAS break-fix chain in VANETs

- Horng et al. [17] (2015, seven algorithms, partial aggregation) broken by Li et al. [18] via malicious-but-passive KGC attack (p.1270, Table 1).
- Cui et al. [19] (2018, first pairing-free ECDLP partial-aggregation scheme) broken by Kumar–Sharma [20] for Type-2 adversary insecurity (p.1270, Table 1).
- Kumar–Sharma [20] broken by Yang et al. [21] for internal-signer and coalition attacks from malicious KGC plus RSU (p.1270, Table 1).
- Zhong et al. [23] (2019, full aggregation) broken independently by Li et al. [30] and Kamil–Ogundoyin [32] for Type-2 signature forgery (p.1270-1271, Table 1).
- Kamil–Ogundoyin [24] (2019, pairing-free full aggregation) breaks Cui et al. [19] for Type-2 forgery, then is broken by Ye et al. [37] for coalition attacks from malicious vehicles (p.1270-1271, Table 1).
- Zhao et al. [25] break Hu et al.'s scheme for forgery attacks, and Hu et al. [26] break Shen et al. [60] for forgery attacks (p.1270, Table 1).
- Table 2 grades every scheme on SA1/SA2 plus S1–S7 with citing cryptanalyses, and states refs [25]–[38] have no cryptanalysis from others since considerably new (p.1272, §4.1).
