# ES-CLAS performance figures
- Headline claim is at least 24.3% lower computation cost than compared schemes [6],[11],[12],[13],[14],[15] (p.1-p.2).
- Single-message signing gains are about 65.5%, 56.3%, 48.8%, 49.1%, 56%, and 24.3% over the same six schemes (p.11).
- Batch aggregate verification of 100 messages gains about 74.6%, 48.9%, 48.9%, 66.9%, 51.7%, and 50% over the same six schemes (p.11).
- Conclusion restates signature overhead down about 24.3% and verification down about 48.9%, with communication down about 19.2% versus scheme [11] (p.12).
- Evaluation assumptions fix an 80-bit supersingular-curve setting for comparators only with fixed per-operation timings (p.10).
- Interpretation: the 24.3% headline is the minimum margin across six comparators rather than a uniform gain over each scheme (p.11).
