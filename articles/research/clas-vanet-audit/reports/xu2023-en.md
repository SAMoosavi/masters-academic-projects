# A Security-Enhanced Conditional Privacy-Preserving Certificateless Aggregate Signature Scheme for Vehicular Ad-Hoc Networks (Xu et al.)

## Bibliographic
- Authors: Xu, Wang, Luo, Long, Zhang, Yan, Chen | Year: 2024 | Venue: IEEE Internet of Things Journal 11(8):13482-13495 | DOI: 10.1109/JIOT.2023.3337136

## Problem
VANET messages require authenticity and integrity with vehicle privacy preservation over open wireless links (p.13482). Many certificateless aggregate signature schemes still face forgery and efficiency gaps such as inability to resist replacement attacks and high computation cost (p.13482). Chen et al. CPP-CLAS [19] in particular cannot resist a public-key replacement attack by an external Type-I adversary (pp.13486–13487). This paper gives an explicit attack algorithm for that flaw and then proposes a security-enhanced pairing-free scheme with invalid-signature identification (p.13482).

## Method
- Setup: additive curve group $G = \langle P \rangle$ of prime order $q$ on $E: y^2 = x^3 + ax + b \pmod p$, master $s$ with $P_{pub} = sP$, tracing secret $t$ with $T_{pub} = tP$, hashes $H_0$–$H_4$, params $\{G, q, T_{pub}, P_{pub}, H_0, H_1, H_2, H_3, H_4, P, G, E\}$ (p.13487, §V).
- PseudonymGen: vehicle picks $f_i \in \mathbb{Z}_p^*$, $AID_{i,1} = f_i P$, $AID_{i,2} = f_i T_{pub}$, $MID_i = VID_i \oplus AID_{i,2}$; TA recovers $VID_i = MID_i \oplus tAID_{i,1}$ and issues $AID_{i,3} = H_0(tAID_{i,1}, \Delta T_i) \oplus VID_i$, $PID_i = \{AID_{i,3}, \Delta T_i\}$ (pp.13487–13488, §V).
- PPKGen: KGC picks $r_i \in \mathbb{Z}_p^*$, $R_i = r_i P$, $h_{1i} = H_1(R_i, P_{pub}, PID_i)$, $d_i = r_i + s h_{1i}$, $PPK_i = (d_i, R_i)$ checked by $d_i P = R_i + h_{1i} P_{pub}$ (p.13488, §V).
- KeyGen: vehicle picks secret $x_i \in \mathbb{Z}_p^*$, $X_i = x_i P$, sets $SK_i = (x_i, d_i)$ and $PK_i = (X_i, R_i)$ (p.13488, §V).
- Sign: vehicle picks $k_i \in \mathbb{Z}_p^*$, $K_i = k_i P$, $h_{2i} = H_2(PID_i, m_i, t_i, K_i, PK_i, P_{pub})$, $h_{3i} = H_3(PID_i, m_i, PK_i, K_i, h_{2i})$, $S_i = k_i + h_{2i} x_i + h_{3i} d_i$, $\sigma_i = (K_i, S_i)$ (p.13488, §V).
- Aggregate: RSU sums $S = \sum_{i=1}^{n} S_i$ and $K = \sum_{i=1}^{n} K_i$ into $\sigma = \{K, S\}$ plus $\{PK_i, t_i, \sigma_i, m_i, PID_i\}$ for the application server (pp.13488–13489, §V).
- Verify: single check $\alpha_i = S_i P - K_i - h_{2i} X_i - h_{3i}(R_i + h_{1i} P_{pub})$, accept iff $\alpha_i = 0$; aggregate check $\alpha = SP - K - \sum_{i=1}^{n} h_{2i} X_i - \sum_{i=1}^{n} h_{3i}(R_i + h_{1i} P_{pub})$, accept iff $\alpha = 0$ (p.13488, §V).
- Caveat: single and aggregate verification list the hash order as $H_1(PID_i, R_i, P_{pub})$ while PPK generation lists $H_1(R_i, P_{pub}, PID_i)$ (p.13488, §V).
- Invalid-ID: on $\alpha^* \neq 0$, AS plus RSU run Binary Quick Search Algorithm 1 to isolate invalid signatures and send bad PIDs to TA for $VID$ reveal (pp.13488–13489, §V).
- Revocation extension only: $S_i = k_i + h_{2i} x_i + h_{3i} d_i + h_{4i} z_i$ with $Z_i = z_i P$ and $h_{4i} = H_4(PID_i, m_i, t_i, Z_i)$ (p.13494, §VII).

## Key findings
- Type-I adversary forges Chen et al. [19] by replacing $PK_l^*$ with $R_l^*$ and $K_l' = z_l^* P - h_{2l}^* P_{pub}$ and forming $V_l^* = u_l^* + z_l^* h_{3l}^*$ that passes aggregate verification (pp.13486–13487, §IV).
- Proposed scheme is EUF-CMA secure against Type-I adversaries in Theorem 1 and Type-II adversaries in Theorem 2 in the random oracle model under ECDLP via Game I and Game II simulation with the Forking Lemma (pp.13489–13491, §V).
- Binary Quick Search Algorithm 1 identifies invalid signatures after a failed aggregate check so valid messages stay usable without full re-verification (p.13488).
- Single sign costs 1 scalar multiplication at about 0.3218 ms, single verify costs 4 multiplications plus 4 additions at about 1.2968 ms, and aggregate verify costs $(2n+2)$ multiplications plus $(2n+2)$ additions at about $0.6484n + 0.6484$ ms on MIRACL timings (p.13492).
- Vehicle-to-RSU overhead is $(PID_i, t_i, PK_i, m_i, \sigma_i)$ at $2|G| + 3|\mathbb{Z}_q^*| + 2|t| = 148$ bytes with $|G| = 40$, $|\mathbb{Z}_q^*| = 20$, $|t| = 4$ (p.13493).
- Comparator note: lower computation and communication than six related schemes [11]–[15],[18] per Tables IV–V and Figs. 3–6, with Kumar et al. [12] aggregate baseline about $2.1866n + 20.4378$ ms cited for contrast (pp.13492–13493).

## Limitations
- Proof is in the random oracle model under ECDLP with no standard-model proof (pp.13489–13491).
- Revocation extension adds a mutual-auth plus key-agreement round that is not costed in the main computation tables (p.13494).
- Traceability and partial-key issue trust TA and KGC with no decentralised tracing except as future work (pp.13487–13494).
- Timing comparison rests on dated Windows 7 plus MIRACL measurements, so absolute millisecond values may not transfer to current platforms (p.13491).
