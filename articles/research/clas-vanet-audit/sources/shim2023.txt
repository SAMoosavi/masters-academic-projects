  ABSTRACT In order to solve security and privacy-preservation issues in VANETs, a number of conditional
  privacy-preservation schemes based on certificateless signature (CLS) schemes and certificateless aggregate
  signature (CLAS) schemes have been proposed. However, most of them were insecure against several types
  of attacks. Recently, Wang et al. and Xiong et al. proposed new conditional privacy-preserving authentication
  schemes based on CLAS and CLS schemes, respectively, for VANETs. We present malicious-but-passive
  KGC attacks on Wang et al.’ scheme, and forgery attacks and key recovery attacks on Xiong et al.’ scheme,
  where KGC or anyone can generate valid signatures on any messages for any vehicles without being
  traced by TA. Our attacks break their unforgeability and traceability. At last, we discuss their causes and
  countermeasures.

  INDEX TERMS Certificateless signature scheme, certificateless aggregate signature, forgery attack, key
  recovery attack, malicious-but-passive KGC attack.
