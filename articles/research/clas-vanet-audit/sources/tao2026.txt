              ES-CLAS: An Efficient Cerificateless Fully
                  Aggregate Signature Scheme for
                   Vehicular Ad Hoc Networks
                                             Qi Tao        and Xiaohui Cui , Senior Member, IEEE

   Abstract—In this work, it reviewed the recent authentication
scheme based on certificateless aggregate signature (CLAS) in
vehicular ad hoc network (VANET). The previously proposed
scheme solved a few of the security problems in VANETs, but
it cann’t resist forgery attacks. Besides, the computation and
communication cost is not sufficiently low that would affect the
efficient delivery of emergency traffic messages. In this paper, we
propose a new efficient cerificateless fully aggregate signature
scheme for VANETs. The proposed scheme used the general
one-way hash function operations instead of the expensive map-
to-point hash functions. It inherits the characteristics of CLAS
by aggregating various message signatures from different vehicles
into a single signature, thus reducing the computation and
communication overhead of the scheme. A lightweight conditional
traceability method is designed to enable the traffic management
department to reveal the real identity of malicious vehicles. Based
on elliptic curve discrete logarithm (ECDL) problem, it proved
that the proposed scheme is existential unforgeability under
the adaptive chosen-message attack and satisfies security goals.
Performance analysis shows that our scheme is more efficient than
similar schemes in terms of computation and communication
overhead. It will reduce at least 24.3% in computation cost than
compared schemes.
